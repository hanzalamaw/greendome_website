Family Umrah 26 July 2026

Umrah with Children from Pakistan

Umrah with children from Pakistan needs extra documents and a calmer schedule. Children need their own passport and B-Form / CRC copies. Do not wait until the week of departure to apply for a child’s passport.

On the trip

Pack snacks they will eat, a light jacket for buses, and a simple explanation of tawaf so it feels like worship. Double or Triple sharing is more realistic than Quad for parents with young children. An 11-day group with shuttle access in Makkah and dinners in Madinah cuts daily logistics.

Tell GreenDome the ages of children when you inquire. The team can advise sharing and meeting points so nobody is left at the airport lobby.

Frequently asked questions

Do children need a passport for Umrah from Pakistan?
Yes. Each child needs their own passport plus B-Form / CRC copies as requested.
Is there an age limit for Umrah?
Infants and children can travel if documents are complete. Confirm seating and sharing with the operator.
Which package is best for a family?
An 11-day group with Triple or Double sharing, visa included, and a group leader — not the cheapest Quad by default.
