What Is Included in an Umrah Package from Pakistan?

Before you book, ask what is included in the Umrah package. Pakistani families should see visa, tickets, both cities’ hotels and ground transport in writing — not only a per-person price.

Green Dome Travel & Tours 11-day inclusions

  • Return air ticket from Pakistan
  • Umrah visa processing, approval and medical insurance
  • 5 nights Voco Makkah (5-star) with free 24/7 Haram shuttle
  • 5 nights Miramar Int. 3 star or similar in Madinah
  • Airport pick and drop
  • Bus between Makkah and Madinah
  • Makkah and Madinah ziyarat
  • Two fully guided Umrah with the group
  • 5 days dinner in Madinah including Albaik
  • Merchandise gift pack, group leader, 24/7 WhatsApp support

Always ask what is not included

Personal spending, extra meals, zamzam extra baggage and optional shopping are usually on you. If a quote is silent on visa or airport transfer, treat those as extra until confirmed.
