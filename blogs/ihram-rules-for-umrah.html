Ihram Rules for Umrah: What Pakistani Pilgrims Must Know

Ihram rules for Umrah are what first-time Pakistani pilgrims worry about on the flight. Ihram is the state you enter with niyyah, not only the white cloths.

What to wear

Men wear two unstitched white cloths and sandals. Women wear modest Islamic dress; there is no required colour, but the face and hands remain uncovered in the usual way of ihram. Use unscented soap. Your group leader will tell you when to change — often before the miqat on the Pakistan–Jeddah or Madinah flight.

What to avoid in ihram

Perfume, cutting hair or nails, and the other well-known restrictions apply until you finish tawaf, sa’i and halq or taqsir. GreenDome groups include two guided Umrah so you are not guessing the sequence at Haram.

Frequently asked questions

When do I wear ihram from Pakistan?
Before crossing the miqat. The group leader will announce the time on your flight or bus.
Can I wear stitched clothes in ihram as a man?
Men wear the two unstitched cloths. Women wear normal modest clothes, not the two-piece ihram.
Can I use perfume in ihram?
No. Pack unscented toiletries for ihram days.
