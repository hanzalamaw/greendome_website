Umrah vs Hajj from Pakistan: What Is the Difference?

Pakistani searchers mix Umrah vs Hajj because both are pilgrimages to Makkah. They are not the same trip, visa or calendar.

Umrah

Umrah can be performed any day of the year. The rites are ihram, tawaf, sa’i and halq or taqsir. Group packages from Pakistan are typically 10–12 days. Green Dome Travel & Tours’ current Umrah is 11 days with published sharing prices and visa included.

Hajj

Hajj has fixed Islamic dates, extra rites (Arafat, Muzdalifah, Mina) and a government quota. It costs more, lasts longer and is harder to book at short notice. Do not buy an “Umrah package” expecting it to count as Hajj.

Most first-time families from Pakistan start with Umrah, then plan Hajj in a later year through the official process.

Frequently asked questions

Can I do Hajj on an Umrah visa?
No. Hajj requires the Hajj process and quota. Umrah is a separate, shorter pilgrimage.
Which is cheaper from Pakistan?
Umrah group packages are typically much cheaper and shorter than Hajj.
Should first-time pilgrims do Umrah or Hajj?
Most Pakistani families do Umrah first so they learn Haram, ihram and travel, then apply for Hajj later.
