First Time Umrah from Pakistan: What to Do

First time Umrah from Pakistan feels heavy until you split it: paperwork at home, then worship in Makkah. A licensed group does the paperwork and walks you through how to perform Umrah.

Before you fly

Meet Umrah requirements for Pakistani citizens (passport, photos, CNIC). Apply through the operator. Learn ihram, tawaf and sa’i in a short briefing. Pack light. Green Dome Travel & Tours’ 11-day group from Pakistan includes visa, tickets, hotels and two guided Umrah.

On the ground

Airport pick-up, hotel check-in, shuttle to Haram, and a group leader on WhatsApp mean you are not negotiating taxis after a night flight. Perform Umrah with the group first. Repeat later if you wish.
