Group Umrah vs Private Umrah from Pakistan

Group Umrah vs private Umrah from Pakistan is a cost and stress question. Private travel gives flexible dates. It also means you handle visa, taxis, ziyarat and language yourself.

Why groups win for first-timers

A group locks flights, hotels, airport pick-up, two guided Umrah and a leader. The per-person price (from PKR 278,000 Quad on GreenDome’s 11-day package) is usually lower than building the same trip privately in a busy month. You can still pray on your own; you are not tied to the group for every salah.

When to go custom or private

Different hotels, extra nights, wheelchair pacing or a family-only date: request a custom quote. You still want a licensed operator for the visa. Tell GreenDome your city in Pakistan, dates and group size.
