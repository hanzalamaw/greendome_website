How to Choose an Umrah Package in Pakistan

How to choose an Umrah package in Pakistan is less about the prettiest flyer and more about five checks: days, hotels, visa, sharing and after-sales contact.

Five checks before you pay

  • Days: 11 days with 5 nights Makkah and 5 nights Madinah is a balanced first trip
  • Hotels: named hotel or a clear “or similar” category, plus shuttle or walking access
  • Visa and flights: both inside the price, from your city in Pakistan
  • Sharing: Quad, Triple or Double that matches who is travelling
  • Support: a WhatsApp number that still answers after the deposit

Frequently asked questions

What is the most important thing when choosing an Umrah package?
A written list of visa, flights, hotels and transport. Price without that list is not a package.
Should I pick the cheapest sharing?
Only if your group is comfortable in a room of four. Elders and small children usually need Triple or Double.
How do I know the operator is real?
Ask for an office address, a published itinerary and a reachable number. Green Dome Travel & Tours is in Karachi and serves pilgrims across Pakistan.
