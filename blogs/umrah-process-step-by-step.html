Umrah Process Step by Step for Pakistani Pilgrims

People in Pakistan search Umrah process and Umrah whole process because they want the journey in order — not only the rites at Haram. Below is the full Umrah process step by step from your home city to Makkah, Madinah and back.

Step 1: Choose an Umrah package and sharing type

Pick an 11-day group from Pakistan if you want visa, flights and hotels in one price. Choose Quad, Triple or Double sharing. Green Dome Travel & Tours’ current Umrah prices are PKR 278,000 / 308,000 / 358,000 per person.

Step 2: Submit documents and pay a booking deposit

Send passport copies, photos, CNIC and any mahram or children’s papers. Pay the deposit to hold seats. Incomplete files are the main reason the Umrah process stalls in the last week.

Step 3: Visa, tickets and travel documents

The operator files the Umrah visa, adds medical insurance and issues return tickets. Original passports come back with your travel documents before departure.

Step 4: Fly from Pakistan and transfer to the hotel

The group flies together. Airport pick-up in Saudi Arabia is included. You check in at Voco Makkah (5 nights, Haram shuttle) and later Madinah (5 nights, Miramar Int. 3 star or similar).

Step 5: Perform Umrah (ihram, tawaf, sa’i)

Enter ihram at the miqat, make tawaf around the Kaaba, perform sa’i between Safa and Marwah, then halq or taqsir. Green Dome Travel & Tours groups include two fully guided Umrah so first-time pilgrims are not left to figure this out alone.

Step 6: Ziyarat in Makkah and Madinah, then return

The package includes Makkah and Madinah ziyarat, bus between the two cities, and 5 days of dinner in Madinah. Then the group flies back to Pakistan.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Umrah process from Pakistan?
Book a package, submit documents, get visa and tickets, fly from Pakistan, perform Umrah in Makkah, visit Madinah, and return with the group.
How long does the whole Umrah process take?
The trip itself is 11 days. Document and visa processing happens before departure — start as soon as you decide to go.
Will someone guide us through Umrah?
Yes. Green Dome Travel & Tours includes two fully guided Umrah with the group and a dedicated group leader.
