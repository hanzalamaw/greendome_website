Passport Validity for Umrah from Pakistan

The first document for Umrah from Pakistan is a valid passport. CNIC is supporting ID for the visa file, not a travel document to Saudi Arabia.

When to renew

If your passport will expire soon after travel, renew it before you apply. Operators cannot file a clean Umrah visa on a passport that is about to lapse. Follow the validity period your operator states for that departure — do not guess from an old WhatsApp forward.

Names and blank pages

The name on the visa must match the passport. Keep blank pages for stamps. Children’s passports are separate; a parent’s passport is not enough. Scan the bio page and take fresh photos before you inquire.

Frequently asked questions

How long should my passport be valid for Umrah?
Use the validity your licensed operator requires for your departure. If expiry is close, renew first, then apply.
Can I do Umrah on CNIC only?
No. You need a valid passport.
Do children share a parent’s passport?
No. Each child needs their own passport for Umrah from Pakistan.
