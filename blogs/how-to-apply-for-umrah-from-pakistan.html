How to Apply for Umrah from Pakistan (Group Package)

How to apply for Umrah from Pakistan is simpler with a group package than trying to file visa, hotels and flights yourself. This is the application path Green Dome Travel & Tours uses for the Pakistan group.

1. Choose your Umrah package and sharing

Open the Umrah page, pick Quad (PKR 278,000), Triple (PKR 308,000) or Double (PKR 358,000), and note the 15 September 2026 tentative departure.

2. Send an inquiry with traveller names

Use the booking form or WhatsApp +92 331 7259177. Give full names as in the passport, city, number of travellers and sharing type.

3. Submit the document file

Passport copies, photos, CNIC, and family papers. This is what the operator needs before they can apply for your Umrah visa.

4. Pay the booking deposit

A deposit holds your seats. Get the remaining payment schedule in writing. Do not treat verbal “starting from” prices as your Umrah package price.

5. Receive visa, tickets and joining instructions

When the visa is approved, you get tickets and the group reporting time for your departure airport. Then you travel and perform Umrah with the group leader.

Frequently asked questions

How do I apply for Umrah from Pakistan?
Choose a licensed group package, send passport documents, pay a deposit, and let the operator file the visa and tickets. Green Dome Travel & Tours takes inquiries by form or WhatsApp.
Can I apply for Umrah visa myself from Pakistan?
Group travellers usually apply through the operator. Private travellers must follow current Saudi/Nusuk rules. Most families prefer the operator to file it.
How early should I apply?
As soon as passports are ready. Group seats and room sharing sell out before departure.
