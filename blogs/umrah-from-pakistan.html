Umrah from Pakistan: Complete Guide for 2026

Umrah is a pilgrimage to Makkah that Pakistani Muslims can perform any day of the year. If you are searching “Umrah” or “Umrah from Pakistan”, you typically need four answers: the price, the visa, the documents, and the step-by-step process from Pakistan to Haram and back.

Umrah from Pakistan in short

Most families book a group Umrah package so visa, flights, hotels and ziyarat are handled together. GreenDome’s current group is an 11-day Rabi ul Awal Umrah from Pakistan, departing 15 September 2026 (tentative), with Quad, Triple and Double sharing.

Umrah prices from Pakistan

Published Umrah package prices: PKR 278,000 Quad, PKR 308,000 Triple, PKR 358,000 Double per person. That includes return tickets, Umrah visa, insurance, 5 nights Makkah, 5 nights Madinah, transfers and guided ziyarat.

What you must have before you apply

  • Valid Pakistani passport with enough remaining validity
  • CNIC copy, photos, and vaccination records as required at the time
  • Mahram documents where applicable, plus children’s passports and B-Form
  • A licensed operator to file the Umrah visa and issue tickets

The Umrah process (overview)

You choose a package, submit documents, pay a booking deposit, receive visa and tickets, fly from Pakistan, enter ihram, perform tawaf and sa’i in Makkah, visit Madinah, then return. A group leader walks first-time pilgrims through each step on the ground.
