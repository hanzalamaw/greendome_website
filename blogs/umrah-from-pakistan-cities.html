Frequently asked questions

Can I book Umrah from Lahore or Islamabad?
Yes. Green Dome Travel & Tours serves pilgrims across Pakistan. Tell us your city when you apply so flights can be arranged with the group.
Is the air ticket included?
Yes. Return air tickets from Pakistan are part of the published Umrah package price.

Not necessarily. Confirm your departure city with the team. Many families fly from their own city; others join a main group sector if that is the better option for their dates.
