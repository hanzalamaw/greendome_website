Before You Apply 13 August 2026

What Do I Need Before Applying for Umrah from Pakistan?

The most practical search Pakistani families make is: what do I need before applying for Umrah? You do not start with ihram towels. You start with a complete file so the operator can apply for visa and seats.

Have these ready before you apply

  • Passport — valid, with blank pages; renew first if expiry is close
  • Photos — recent passport-size, white background if asked
  • CNIC copy — for every adult
  • Children’s papers — passport + B-Form / CRC
  • Mahram documents — if they apply to anyone in your group
  • Health records — whatever is required for that season
  • Booking deposit — to hold Quad, Triple or Double sharing
  • Correct names — same spelling on passport and booking form

What you do not need to arrange yourself on a group package

If you book GreenDome’s Umrah package, you do not separately arrange the Umrah visa, return tickets, Makkah/Madinah hotels, airport transfer or ziyarat buses. Those are included in the Umrah package price. You still must provide the documents above.

Do this one week before you inquire

Scan the passport bio page, take fresh photos, and write each traveller’s name exactly as in the passport. Put everyone’s papers in one envelope. Missing one child’s photograph can hold the whole family application.

Apply with a complete Umrah file

Frequently asked questions

What do I need before applying for Umrah from Pakistan?
Valid passports, photos, CNIC copies, children’s B-Form if needed, mahram papers where applicable, health records as required, and a booking deposit with a licensed operator.
Can I apply for Umrah without a passport?
No. Get or renew the passport first, then apply. CNIC is not enough.
Should I buy tickets before the visa?
On a group package, the operator issues tickets with the visa. Do not buy separate tickets unless you are travelling privately.
