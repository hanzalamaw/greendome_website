How to Perform Umrah: Simple Steps for First-Time Pakistanis

How to perform Umrah is the part of the process that happens in Makkah. Pakistani first-timers should learn this sequence before the flight, then follow the group leader at Haram.

1. Ihram

Men wear two unstitched white cloths. Women wear modest Islamic dress without a specific colour. Make the intention (niyyah) and recite talbiyah. Enter ihram before or at the miqat — your group leader will tell you the timing on the Pakistan–Jeddah/Madinah flight or on the bus.

2. Tawaf

Walk seven circuits around the Kaaba, starting from the Black Stone corner. Keep the Kaaba on your left. If the mataf is crowded, stay calm and follow the group.

3. Sa’i

Walk seven times between Safa and Marwah. This completes the main walking rites of Umrah.

4. Halq or taqsir

Men shave or shorten the hair. Women cut a small amount. After this, ihram restrictions end.

Frequently asked questions

How do I perform Umrah step by step?
Ihram and niyyah, tawaf around the Kaaba, sa’i between Safa and Marwah, then halq or taqsir. A group leader guides first-time pilgrims.
Do I need to memorise long duas?
No. Learn the sequence and basic talbiyah. You may recite Qur’an or any dua you know during tawaf and sa’i.
Is Umrah the same as Hajj?
No. Hajj has fixed dates and extra rites. Umrah can be performed any time of the year on your package dates.
