Umrah Requirements for Pakistani Citizens (2026)

If you searched Umrah requirements for Pakistani or “Umrah requirement for Pakistani”, this is the checklist operators actually use before they can apply for your visa and ticket.

Basic Umrah requirements for Pakistani citizens

  • Pakistani passport with enough remaining validity and blank pages
  • Umrah visa (filed through a licensed operator for group packages)
  • Recent passport-size photographs as specified
  • CNIC copy matching the passport name as closely as possible
  • Medical insurance (included in Green Dome Travel & Tours packages)
  • Vaccination or health records required at the time of travel

Extra Umrah requirements for families

  • Women should confirm mahram rules that apply to their age and visa type for that departure
  • Children need their own passports plus B-Form / CRC copies
  • Elders should declare mobility or medicine needs when they inquire

Requirements that delay applications

Expired passports, unclear photos, and different spellings on CNIC vs passport are the most common problems. Visa and health rules can change — follow the exact list your operator sends for your departure date, not a screenshot from last year.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Umrah requirements for Pakistani citizens?
A valid passport, photos, CNIC, health records as required, Umrah visa, and mahram/children’s documents where applicable. A licensed operator files the visa for group packages.
Is a CNIC enough for Umrah from Pakistan?
No. You need a valid passport. CNIC is supporting ID for the visa file, not a travel document to Saudi Arabia.
Do I need vaccination for Umrah from Pakistan?
Health rules change. Your operator will tell you what is required for your exact travel date.
