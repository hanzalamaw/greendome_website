How Many Days for Umrah from Pakistan?

Pakistani families ask how many days for Umrah because leave from work and school is limited. You can complete the rites in a few hours, but the trip also includes flights, rest, Madinah and ziyarat.

Why 11 days is a practical length

GreenDome’s group is 11 days and 10 nights: 5 nights in Makkah and 5 nights in Madinah. That gives time for two guided Umrah, Haram prayers without rushing, and ziyarat in both cities. Shorter 5–7 day packages often cut Madinah or stack long travel on the same day as tawaf.

Can Umrah be done in fewer days?

Yes, if you only want Makkah and fly straight back. First-time pilgrims and elders usually regret that. Equal nights in both holy cities is the standard Pakistani families look for in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for Umrah from Pakistan?
Eleven days with five nights in each city is a comfortable length for first-time families.
How long do the Umrah rites take?
Tawaf and sa’i can be completed in a few hours, but you still need flight days, rest and Madinah ziyarat.
Is a 7-day Umrah package enough?
It can work for experienced travellers. Elders and first-timers are usually better on 10–11 days.
