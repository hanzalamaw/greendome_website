Cheap Umrah Package in Pakistan: What You Actually Get

Searches for a cheap Umrah package in Pakistan are usually about stretching a family budget. A low number is only useful if visa, flights, hotels and ziyarat are inside it. Many Facebook ads quote a hotel-only or “starting from” figure that is not the real Umrah cost.

A complete cheap Umrah package still has a floor

Green Dome Travel & Tours’ 11-day group from Pakistan is priced at PKR 278,000 Quad, PKR 308,000 Triple and PKR 358,000 Double per person. That is the lowest sharing option with return tickets, Umrah visa, insurance, Makkah and Madinah hotels, transfers and guided ziyarat included.

What cheap ads often exclude

  • Umrah visa and medical insurance
  • Airport pick-up in Saudi Arabia
  • Bus between Makkah and Madinah
  • Ziyarat and a group leader
  • Meals you assumed were included

Add those back and the “cheap” package is often more expensive than a published group price. Ask for a written inclusion list before you pay a deposit.
