Umrah Visa for Pakistani Citizens: How It Works

An Umrah visa for Pakistani passport holders is filed through official channels (including Nusuk) by a licensed operator for group packages. You cannot fly for Umrah on CNIC alone.

Umrah visa requirements (Pakistan)

  • Valid passport
  • Photographs as specified
  • Personal details matching CNIC
  • Health / vaccination records required at the time
  • Mahram documentation where applicable

Is Umrah visa included in the package price?

Yes, on GreenDome’s group. Umrah visa processing, approval handling and medical insurance are inside the Umrah package price from Pakistan. You still send documents on time.

How long does Umrah visa take?

It depends on the season and whether your file is complete. Apply early. Peak months and incomplete photos cause the longest waits.

Do not confuse Umrah visa with Hajj. Hajj has a separate quota and process. Umrah is the shorter pilgrimage you can perform on the dates of your group package.
